Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a password (stored only in hashed form). When you use the app, we store the expenses you enter, including the amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, to show your expenses and spending summaries, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can delete your expenses at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and its data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
